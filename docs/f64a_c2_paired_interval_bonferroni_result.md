# F64A C2 conservative spread interval: held-out CPU result

The separately frozen [specification](f64a_c2_paired_interval_bonferroni_v1.json), SHA-256 `533ec7babd4635c757745d6c1cf6e4464ceaf046969ccdc9e5e2e338ed4260eb`, was screened on development data and then run on its reserved held-out cohort. The [runner](../src/analysis/f64a_c2_paired_interval_bonferroni.py), SHA-256 `300b2aa03079c9e162f081cc7cb3398df8ec30453c5fd183d63f85272af440c8`, forms separate chi-square intervals for the baseline and HREX late standard deviations at 97.5% coverage each, then subtracts their bounds. Under independent, normally distributed realizations within each arm, the union bound gives **at least 95% coverage** for the SD difference regardless of within-pair correlation. Paired rows are retained; incomplete pairs remain in the attempted denominator. The paired Student-t movement interval is unchanged. No SCC access or GPU work occurred.

All eight complete/MCAR cases passed the frozen ≥92% conditional-coverage screen in [development](../results/cpu_calibration/f64a_c2_paired_interval_bonferroni_v1/development.json) (SHA-256 `bbdc8cf43101cb80f5f9e5c1a6ae6fb32a383c377985c055161e5eb08f1fe6f6`) and [held-out](../results/cpu_calibration/f64a_c2_paired_interval_bonferroni_v1/heldout.json) (SHA-256 `5b9504617eb6cbc3e4ce148eb6604f91dbb30f7892cd29cd8a8b58929e743248`) cohorts. Held-out spread coverage was 99.9–100% across these cases, while movement coverage was 94.4–96.0%. The four outcome-dependent-missingness scenarios remain descriptive failure probes. At 10 attempted pairs, two to seven of the 1,000 MCAR intervals were unavailable because fewer than five pairs completed; complete-case denominators and failures are saved in the reports.

**Precision remains poor.** In the constructed units, mean held-out spread interval width was 2.31 for 10 complete pairs and 2.87–2.91 for 10-pair MCAR, versus a true SD contrast of −0.25. At 33 attempted pairs it was 1.04 for complete pairs and 1.18–1.19 for MCAR. This conservative fallback passes a normal-model coverage check, but it is too broad to justify a C2 useful-effect or repeat-count decision from these trial sizes. Normality of protein repeat estimates is also untested. The earlier percentile, BCa, and Wishart spread candidates remain rejected by their own screens; this result does not authorize production inference or an overall HREX-benefit claim.
