# F64A C2 paired Wishart spread interval: development stop

The separately frozen [normal-model specification](f64a_c2_paired_interval_wishart_v1.json), SHA-256 `f4ab2ed047a23b9af26137c5c6cfdf9aa76fd2429e4b26d05ae3844740264b48`, was run locally on **development data only**. The [runner](../src/analysis/f64a_c2_paired_interval_wishart.py), SHA-256 `2b5c7aea0e60622dd971dfb849c5837c1e815de65f7f73d61b6627f177ab942e`, derives an interval for the difference of late-arm standard deviations from an inverse-Wishart covariance pivot. It keeps complete paired rows, reports missing attempts, and leaves the paired Student-t movement interval unchanged. This relies on a bivariate normal model for late values; it is not a distribution-free protein interval.

The frozen development screen required at least 92% conditional coverage in all eight complete/MCAR cases. With 200 trials per scenario and 1,999 pivot draws per available interval, seven of eight cases passed. The **33-pair, negative-0.6-correlation, complete-data case covered 179/200 = 89.5%**, below the threshold. The 10-pair spread coverages were 92.0–94.0%; the 33-pair spread coverages were 89.5–98.0%. All development details and availability counts are in [development.json](../results/cpu_calibration/f64a_c2_paired_interval_wishart_v1/development.json), SHA-256 `ee1efe2ddd817c506375255b0ab6d0b535949e5a0d5277e5b4c5d080de7a72c3`.

The reserved held-out cohort was **not run**. This candidate is not selected. A conservative marginal-variance bound can avoid dependence modeling under an exact normal assumption, but its width and behavior under nonnormal protein repeats must be assessed separately. No spread method, production pair count, or scientific C2 result follows.
