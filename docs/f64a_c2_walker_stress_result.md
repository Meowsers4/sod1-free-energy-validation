# F64A C2 synthetic walker-lineage uncertainty stress v1

Completed locally on 2026-10-03. The longer-block joint bootstrap **passed the frozen IID and moderate-correlation diagnostics but failed the long-correlation diagnostic**. It therefore does not qualify a general C2 joint variance, noise-adjusted `Q`, or production uncertainty. No SCC access or GPU job occurred.

The frozen [specification](f64a_c2_walker_stress_v1.json), SHA-256 `66dbcc637b9e1a0f38b9d2e708955273d89f9e9e56e7205fa12e352c02fd0dda`, fixed 20 harmonic states, 256 observations per early/late block, 24 synchronized stationary-bootstrap replicates per seed, and mean resampling block length 96. Four development seeds per regime also ran lengths 32 and 160 as *descriptive sensitivity checks*. Thirty-two separate held-out seeds per regime used only the registered primary length. The [runner](../src/analysis/f64a_c2_walker_stress.py), SHA-256 `98fa7d2c7f684a272c8aaf840dbb2f289138feb2294d1fa12c5303ebe7ed6dfa`, records the state-to-walker permutation at every time, verifies identity and adjacent-swap timing, and resamples complete synchronized 20-state rows. Contiguous bootstrap runs retain the original walker history internally; block joins interrupt it.

The swaps are **coordinate-independent**. This makes each state's equilibrium harmonic marginal and zero free-energy endpoint known, while creating migrating walker memory. It is not a Metropolis HREX simulation and does not reproduce the C2 protein dynamics. The regimes are IID, swapped walkers with AR(1) coefficient 0.9 and 25% shared innovation, an otherwise identical coefficient-0.98 case, and a nonstationary linear-shift probe. The frozen stationary pass rule required at least 30/32 complete fits, 27/32 nominal-95% intervals for signed early-to-late movement containing zero, and mean estimated joint variance between 0.5 and 2 times the empirical across-seed variance. Drift had no pass rule.

The development set had only four seeds per regime, so its between-seed variance ratios are unstable. For the long-correlation regime, its variance ratio rose from 0.35 at mean block length 32 to 0.63 at 160; this direction suggests block-length sensitivity, **not** a held-out rescue or a validated choice of 160. The held-out result at 96 was frozen before inspection. All 32 seeds per regime completed. The drift case's mean movement was −0.709 kT across seeds; a stationary-bootstrap coverage claim is undefined for that nonstationary construction. The result tests one simplified swap mechanism and one primary block length, with finite sample and bootstrap Monte Carlo error.
